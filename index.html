Prayer of Thanksgiving & The Lord’s Prayer

Leader

Generous God, we thank you for every gift you have entrusted to us, and especially for the children who live and grow among us. Thank you for their questions and curiosity, their imagination and energy, their discoveries and surprises. Receive the gifts we offer today and use them to build a community in which children are treasured, nurtured, protected, and given room to grow. May all that we give and all that we do help people of every age grow in wisdom and in your grace. In Jesus’ name we pray. Amen.

Leader

Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil.

For thine is the kingdom and the power and the glory, forever. Amen.
